Clothes Donation Guide

Where to Donate Clothes
in Johor Bahru

Free drop-off points across JB — open 24/7, no appointment, no sorting required.

If you have old clothes, shoes, or household linens piling up at home, donating them in Johor Bahru has never been simpler. Yellow Bin operates a network of textile recycling drop-off points across JB — all completely free to use, open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, with no appointment or registration required. Just bag your items and drop them in.

What You Can Donate

Yellow Bin accepts a wide range of textiles and soft goods. You do not need to sort items before donating — drop everything into one bag and we handle it from there.

Clothing & Textiles
T-shirts, pants, dresses, jackets, underwear, socks — all sizes and ages welcome.

Shoes & Accessories
Sneakers, heels, boots, bags, belts, scarves, hats — accepted in any condition.

Linens & Soft Furnishings
Towels, bedsheets, curtains, cushion covers, soft toys and blankets.

Tip: Place items in a bag before dropping in. No need to sort — we handle everything from there. Make sure clothing is dry before donating.

How to Donate

The process takes under two minutes:

  • Collect the clothes, shoes, or linens you no longer need
  • Place them in a plastic bag and tie it securely
  • Find your nearest Yellow Bin drop-off point
  • Drop the bag into the bin — that's it

No forms, no queues, no collection windows. The bins are accessible around the clock.

What Happens to Your Donated Clothes

Once your items are collected from the bin, they are transported to Yellow Bin's sorting centre in Johor Bahru. At the sorting centre, every piece is assessed and given the best possible second life:

  • Grade A and B clothing is redistributed to welfare homes, underprivileged communities, and charitable organisations across the region
  • Worn shoes, even those in poor condition, are recycled into padding and insulation materials
  • Scrap and rejected textiles are sold to workshops as industrial rags or affordable used clothing — keeping every piece out of landfill

Nothing usable goes to waste. Yellow Bin's approach keeps textiles circulating in the economy rather than ending up in Malaysian landfills.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I donate clothes in JB?

At any Yellow Bin drop-off point across Johor Bahru. Locations cover JB City, Tebrau, Skudai, Austin Heights, and Larkin. Use the Find a Bin page to locate the nearest one with directions.

Is it free to donate?

Yes, donating at a Yellow Bin is completely free. No registration, no fee, no appointment needed.

Are the bins open 24 hours?

Yes. Yellow Bin drop-off points are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Drop off your items at any time that suits you.

Do clothes need to be in good condition?

Shoes and accessories are accepted in any condition. For clothing, items should be dry and free from holes, yellowing, pilling, or strong odors — they do not need to be freshly laundered, just dry and in reasonable shape.

Can I get a Yellow Bin for my building or school?

Yes. Yellow Bin partners with schools, condominiums, shopping centres, and workplaces to place bins in convenient community locations — at zero cost to the host. We manage collection and maintenance. Visit the Partner With Us page to submit a request, or message us on WhatsApp.
